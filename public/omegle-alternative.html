An Omegle alternative for one-to-one chat

Omegle shut down in November 2023. If you are looking for sites like Omegle, PineappleChat at pineapplechat.com is a free 18+ website where you talk one-to-one with people who are online, by text, right in your browser. If you meet someone you like, you can keep them as a friend with an inbox.

An honest note PineappleChat is new and small, so sometimes only a few people are online. A clearly labeled PineappleChat Bot is always in the People list. It is a bot, not a person, and it keeps you company until someone real joins. We do not show fake user counts.

What happened to Omegle?

Omegle launched in 2009 and paired random strangers for text and video chats. Its founder closed the site on 8 November 2023, saying that running it was no longer sustainable. Omegle is no longer available. PineappleChat is a separate site and is not affiliated with Omegle.

What you can do on PineappleChat

  • Start a one-to-one chat without an account: just a username, age (18+), and location.
  • See who is online and filter the People list by gender, age, country, and city.
  • Register free with email to add friends and use an inbox that holds messages when they are offline. See friend chat.
  • Turn on Hidden mode to browse without appearing in the list until you message someone.
  • Leave, mute, block, or report at any time. Automated filters block many links and unsafe phrases.
  • Use it on your phone or computer. It runs in the browser, so there is no app to install.

How to start

  1. Open pineapplechat.com.
  2. Enter a username, your age (18+), and your location, then accept the Terms & Safety Agreement.
  3. Tap someone in People to start a one-to-one chat.

Omegle alternative FAQ

Is Omegle still working?

No. Omegle shut down on 8 November 2023 and is no longer running. PineappleChat is a separate website and is not affiliated with Omegle.

Is PineappleChat free?

Yes. Live chat is free, and so is a registered account with friends and inbox.

Do I need an account to chat?

No. Enter a username, age (18+), and location, then tap someone who is online. Register with email only if you want friend requests, an inbox, and a saved profile.

Does PineappleChat have video chat like Omegle?

No. PineappleChat is a text chat website. There is no live webcam roulette.

Is PineappleChat only for adults?

Yes. PineappleChat is for people 18 and over only. You confirm your age and accept the Terms & Safety Agreement before you chat.

What if nobody else is online?

PineappleChat is a new site, so the People list can be quiet. A clearly labeled PineappleChat Bot is always in the list. It is a bot, not a person, and you can chat with it until someone real joins.
